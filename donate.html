Donation Help

Donation FAQs

Everything you need to know before you give.

Is my donation tax deductible?

Yes. Stackly Foundation is registered under 12A & 80G, so eligible donations qualify for deduction under Indian income tax law. Tick the 80G box and add your PAN to receive the receipt.

When will I receive my receipt?

An acknowledgement is emailed immediately, and the official 80G receipt follows within seven working days of a successful payment.

Can I cancel or change a monthly gift?

Yes, at any time. Write to us through the contact page and we will pause, change or cancel your monthly gift within two working days.

Is my payment information safe?

Payments are handled by a certified payment partner. We never see or store your card, UPI PIN or bank details.

Can I choose which program my gift supports?

Absolutely. Pick a program in the form above. If you choose “Where it is needed most”, we allocate your gift to the program with the most urgent funding gap.
